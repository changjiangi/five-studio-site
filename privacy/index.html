Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Five Studio (fivvestudio.com)

Five Studio ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what limited information is collected when you visit our website at fivvestudio.com, contact us via email, or play our games distributed on PC platforms such as Steam.

1. Information We Collect on This Website

Our website is a static informational site. We do not use advertising trackers, behavioral profiling, or third-party marketing cookies.

  • Direct Communications: If you email us (for example at hello@fivvestudio.com, press@fivvestudio.com, biz@fivvestudio.com, or support@fivvestudio.com), we receive your email address and the contents of your message solely to respond to your inquiry or support request.
  • Hosting & Font Delivery: Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads web fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may process standard technical connection data (such as IP address and browser user-agent) in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

2. Information in Our Games (PC / Steam)

When you purchase or play Five Studio games through third-party storefronts such as Steam (operated by Valve Corporation), platform features including cloud saves, achievements, leaderboards, purchase processing, and crash diagnostics are governed by that platform's own privacy policy.

Unless explicitly disclosed inside a specific game before collection, our games do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) or sell player data to third parties. If any future online or multiplayer title requires telemetry or account data, we will update this policy and clearly inform players in-game.

3. How We Use & Share Data

  • To answer player support requests, bug reports, press inquiries, and business messages.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention & Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

We keep email correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve your request or maintain business records. Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EEA, UK, and California), you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data you have sent us.

To exercise any privacy right, please email support@fivvestudio.com with the subject line "Privacy Request".

5. Children's Privacy

Our website and direct communication channels are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data to us, please contact support@fivvestudio.com and we will promptly delete it.

6. External Links

Our website links to external platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and Steam. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or platforms.
